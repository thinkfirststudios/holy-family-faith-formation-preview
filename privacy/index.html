What this website collects

This website has no forms, no accounts and no comments. It does not ask you for anything, and it uses no analytics, no advertising or tracking tools, and no cookies of its own.

Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep routine technical records of visits, such as an IP address, the page requested and the time. [WEB HOST AND HOW LONG IT KEEPS THESE RECORDS — CONFIRM]

Videos

Some pages show videos from YouTube, in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. When such a page loads, and when you play a video, your browser connects to YouTube, and YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that connection. The Register page has no videos.
