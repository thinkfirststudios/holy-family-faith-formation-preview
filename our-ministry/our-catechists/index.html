Missionary Disciples — Our Catechists

A MISSIONARY DISCIPLE is one who: Introduces others to Jesus • Accompanies others on their Way • Journeys with them to the Sacraments • Inspires, forms, equips and sends others on mission to share Jesus and make disciples!

May God bless all of us as we work to live as missionary disciples! Please pray for those who actively serve in our ministry, they are also known as Catechists:

[CATECHIST NAMES ARE HELD BACK UNTIL EACH PERSON HAS GIVEN PERMISSION — PARISH TO CONFIRM]

Pope Francis states that the call to be a disciple in mission to the world comes with each person’s baptism as priest (one who prays), prophet (one who teaches), and king (one who serves) in which he or she shares in Christ’s role and mission (General Directory on Catechesis, 81).
