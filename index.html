Welcome

The Ministry of Faith Formation for Children serves 1st through 5th children, working together with the family in formation and preparing those seeking 1st Reconciliation and 1st Holy Communion. This journey is so important that we ask our parents/family adults to join their children in their journey in faith.

The Faith Formation Ministries serve all the baptized. We work collaboratively because our journey in faith continues after communion, and so we commit to serve lifelong faith formation, centered in the Church and strengthened by the domestic church – the family.

We offer 4 different classes that are open to children in grades TK-5

SUNDAY PRESCHOOL(ages 4–5)

Children (ages 4-5) will build a foundational understanding of the Catholic faith and basic moral values such as kindness, sharing, compassion, and love for others through: Song, Stories, Craft & Prayer

About this class

YEAR 1(grades 1–5)

Children (in grades 1-5) will build a foundational understanding of God, learn basic prayers, and understand the importance of loving God and others.

About this class

YEAR 2 & Sacramental Preparation

Children who have completed Year 1 will continue to grow in their relationship with God while preparing for the Sacraments of Reconciliation and First Holy Communion.

About this class

YOUNG DISCIPLES

We invite children who have already received the Sacraments to return, where they can dive deeper into the core concepts of the Catholic Church.

About this class

Registration Fees:

  • Basic Registration Fee: $150 per year, due at registration.
  • Sacramental Fee: $60, required for second-year students, in addition to tuition.
    Payments can be made online, with an option to spread the tuition across four monthly installments.

Note: Before registering, proof of your child’s baptism is required. Please have your child’s Baptism Certificate ready to upload, including baptisms performed here at Holy Family Church.
