FAITH FORMATION FOR CHILDREN
Serves children in 1st through 5th grade, our ministry partners with families to nurture faith development and prepare children for the sacraments of First Reconciliation and First Holy Communion.

Program Overview:
Faith Formation is an ongoing journey. Preparation for the sacraments is a two-year process, with First Reconciliation and First Holy Communion preparation open to all baptized children. Classes meet once a week, either on Sundays or Wednesdays, starting in late August and continuing through early May. Breaks are observed during Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Easter.
